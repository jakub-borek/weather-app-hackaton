How it works

Bring your own agent. We keep the rules.

Your Grok Bot

“We need 20 MacBooks, 30 Lenovo, 10 HP.”
Any buyer agent connects to our marketplace API. Ours runs on Grok Bot.

→

Our marketplace

Turns it into one request for quote (RFQ) and sends it to every seller that stocks the products. Logs every offer.

→

Seller agents

One Grok agent per shop. Sees only its own stock and prices. Offer → counter → final.

Rules live in code, not in the prompt

  • Price floor per product
  • Stock limit
  • Max 3 rounds
  • Seller sees only its own data

A human clicks Accept

→ real order in Mercur at the negotiated price, split per seller. No agent can accept.
