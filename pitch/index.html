How it works

Bring your own agent. We keep the rules.

Your Grok Bot

“We need 20 MacBooks and 10 HP, budget 130k.”
Any buyer agent connects to our marketplace. Ours runs on Grok Bot.

→

Our marketplace

Turns it into one request for quote (RFQ) and sends it to every seller that stocks the products. Logs every offer.

→

Seller agents

One Grok agent per shop. Sees only its own stock and prices. Offer → counter → final.

Rules live in code, not in the prompt

  • Price floor per product
  • Stock limit
  • Max 3 rounds
  • Seller sees only its own data

A human says yes

Clicks Accept in the store, or approves in the chat with their bot. → real order in Mercur at the negotiated price, split per seller.
